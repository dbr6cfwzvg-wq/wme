Wharton’s Home for Media and Entertainment

The Wharton Media & Entertainment Club is a community of MBA students passionate about the business behind film, television, music, gaming, sports, and digital media. We connect members with industry leaders, alumni, and employers through speaker events, treks, and our annual NYC conference, while giving students a place to explore careers across a rapidly evolving landscape. Whether you’re aiming to break into the industry or simply love great storytelling, our club is where Wharton’s business expertise meets the creative world.
