Media & Entertainment NYC Trek

📍 New York City Thursday, November 12 – Friday, November 13, 2026
--Days
--Hours
--Minutes
--Seconds

Join Wharton’s Media & Entertainment Club for our annual NYC Trek, a two-day opportunity to connect with leading companies and professionals shaping the media and entertainment landscape.

Participants will have the opportunity to:

  • 🎥

    Visit leading companies across film and TV, music, sports, and more to learn about their businesses and the evolving industry landscape.

  • 🎙️

    Hear directly from industry professionals about emerging trends, business challenges, and the future of media and entertainment.

  • 🤝

    Connect with Wharton alumni, fellow M&E enthusiasts, and students from other MBA programs through a cross-school networking mixer with the CBS and NYU M&E clubs.

Last year, ~25 students attended the trek, visiting companies including YouTube, Spotify, and Paramount. This year, we’re expanding our company lineup to offer an even broader look at the industry and more opportunities to build your M&E network!
